01 / RESEARCH

About my work

Understanding innovative entrepreneurship.
How new firms emerge, develop and grow.

Innovative entrepreneurship has always been my main field of research, with a focus on founders' human capital, venture capital and new firm growth. A complementary strand of my work examines network industries — their regulation, innovation dynamics and technology adoption — and their interconnections with innovative entrepreneurship, particularly through digital platforms that operate as network firms reshaping traditional industry boundaries. More recently, this lens has extended to artificial intelligence itself, whose multi-layered network externalities — from data and infrastructure to platforms and ecosystems — make it a network industry in its own right, a theme central to my book The AI Entrepreneur.
